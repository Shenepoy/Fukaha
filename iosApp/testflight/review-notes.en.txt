No login or demo account is required.

To test core functionality:
1. Open Fukaha and use the sample-link control beside the link field, or paste a public social-media URL.
2. Keep Ask each time selected, open the share screen, and copy or share the cleaned link.
3. Change a platform's preferred embed source in settings and prepare a link from that platform.
4. In Safari, share a public webpage using Fukaha or the Clean with Fukaha action. The extension accepts URLs and text containing URLs.

The app and its two extensions use group.com.shenepoy.fukaha to share settings and cached media.
Optional media downloading requires a user-configured compatible HTTPS Cobalt server. The cleaned-link and embed-link flows work without that configuration. No server credentials are bundled.
Preview results depend on third-party providers and the receiving app. No in-app purchase is required.

Before external beta review, the owner must complete the real review-contact details, feedback email, and privacy information in App Store Connect. No contact information is embedded in this file.
